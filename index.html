Nairen Cao（曹乃仁）

About me

I am an Associate Professor in the Institute for Theoretical Computer Science at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. The institute is part of the School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence. I received my Ph.D. from Georgetown University under the supervision of Jeremy T. Fineman and Ophir Frieder. Previously, I was a Faculty Fellow at NYU Tandon School of Engineering, hosted by Martin Farach-Colton, and a Postdoctoral Researcher at Boston College, advised by Hsin-Hao Su.

Here are my academic CV and industry resume (PDF, updated September 6, 2026).

Research Interests

  • Design and analysis of algorithms for combinatorial optimization
  • Graph and clustering algorithms, including parallel and distributed algorithms
  • Applications to LLM systems, including LLM evaluation and KV-cache optimization
